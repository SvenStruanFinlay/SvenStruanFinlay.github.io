About me

Engineer, product thinker, team lead

I'm a senior product engineer at Marshmallow, a Series C insurtech in London, where I joined in 2021 as one of the first hundred employees. I started as a junior, was promoted in 2022 and again in 2026, and now lead the architecture and technical direction of the telematics team.

The work I enjoy most is the kind that starts with no spec and no clear answer. I write the PRD and the technical design, break the build into milestones that other engineers own, and work with the partners outside the codebase, such as hardware and logistics companies, until it ships.

I also deputise for my engineering manager: running delivery syncs and retros, mentoring engineers through promotions and role changes, and moving the team to working AI first with Claude Code.

Before Marshmallow I worked in health tech, building machine learning models with the NHS and prototyping at Medidata. Healthcare is where I'd like to bring this way of working next.
